# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 18, 2026*

This policy explains how **Composio Magic** ("the App"), a personal, non-commercial tool operated by the GitHub user [Platube](https://github.com/Platube), handles data obtained from TikTok.

## 1. Data we access
With your authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the App can access only the following scopes:
- `user.info.basic`: open ID, avatar, display name.
- `user.info.profile`: profile link, bio, verification status.
- `user.info.stats`: follower, following, likes and video counts.
- `video.list`: your public videos.
- `video.upload` / `video.publish`: uploading and posting videos you choose to publish.

## 2. How we use it
Data is used solely to display the account owner's own statistics and videos and to publish content at the owner's explicit request. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

## 3. Sharing
We do not sell or share your data. Data transits through [Composio](https://composio.dev), the integration platform that stores the OAuth access tokens needed to call TikTok's API on your behalf, and through TikTok itself. No other third party receives it.

## 4. Storage and retention
The App does not keep its own database. OAuth tokens are held by Composio until you disconnect the App. Statistics are fetched on demand and not stored by us.

## 5. Your rights and revoking access
You can revoke access at any time in TikTok: *Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions*. Revoking access invalidates the tokens. You may also request deletion of any data related to you via the contact below.

## 6. Children
The App is not directed to anyone under 18.

## 7. Changes
We may update this policy. The current version is always published on this page.
